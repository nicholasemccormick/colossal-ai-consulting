§ 01 — The Problem
Maintenance is not just an expense. It is one of the biggest places independent property managers lose time, margin, and operating capacity.
§ 02 — Common Profit Leaks
01

Vendor Chasing

Staff spend hours every week following up on availability, status, and completion.

  • Slow dispatch
  • Inconsistent follow-up
  • Delayed completion
02

Status Calls

Residents and owners ask for updates because communication is fragmented or manual.

  • Repeated inbound calls
  • Manual email updates
  • Staff interruptions
03

Work-Order Friction

Requests arrive through too many channels and require staff to interpret, route, and prioritize them.

  • Duplicate requests
  • Manual triage
  • Missed escalations
04

Invoice Delays

Manual approvals and missing documentation slow payment and cloud true maintenance costs.

  • Missing invoices
  • Slow approvals
  • Poor cost visibility
05

Owner Friction

Owners receive inconsistent information and question costs when documentation is weak.

  • Reactive updates
  • Disputes over spend
  • Low transparency
06

Scaling Pressure

As doors grow, maintenance workload grows faster than the team can absorb.

  • More admin labor
  • Slower response
  • Lower margins
