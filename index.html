§ 01 — The Problem
Search used to return ten options. AI returns one answer naming two or three businesses. If you are not in it, you did not lose the deal — you were never considered.
§ 02 — Why AI Does Not Name You
01

Undefined Entity

Answer engines must confirm what your business is before recommending it. Inconsistent names, descriptions, and profiles make you unclassifiable.

  • Conflicting descriptions
  • Unverified profiles
  • No structured identity
02

No Third-Party Signal

Brands are roughly 6.5x more likely to be cited through third-party sources than through their own website.

  • Few outside mentions
  • Absent from directories
  • No press or podcasts
03

Thin Organic Footprint

97% of sources cited in Google AI Overviews come from the top 20 organic results. Weak SEO closes the door before AEO starts.

  • Low rankings
  • Few referring domains
  • Shallow content
04

Unextractable Content

Models lift short, direct answers. Long unbroken copy without clear question-and-answer structure rarely gets quoted.

  • No direct answers
  • Vague headings
  • Unattributed claims
05

No Video Presence

YouTube mentions show the strongest measured correlation with AI visibility of any single signal — and almost no small business competes there.

  • No video footprint
  • No transcripts
  • Untapped channel
06

Zero Measurement

Most businesses have never checked what AI says about them, so they cannot tell a working program from a model update.

  • No baseline
  • No prompt tracking
  • No competitor view
