The problem

Meta rewards creative volume. People are the constraint.

Clever targeting stopped being the lever years ago. What moves an account now is how much varied creative you can put in front of the market — which makes your bottleneck production speed, not strategy.

01

The retainer is slow

You're paying monthly and waiting on creative rounds. By the time an ad is live, the angle has moved on without it.

02

Fatigue outruns production

Winners wear out. If the replacement only starts once the current ad dies, there's always a gap where performance drops.

03

Kill decisions are guesses

Without a fixed threshold, ads get cut on a feeling — killing winners early and funding losers too long, often in the same week.

What we build

Four agents and a warehouse that make each other smarter.

Every part writes to one database. That's the difference between a system that learns and an automation that fires once and forgets — it can see what it published and what that produced.

Research

Mines how your buyers actually talk

Pulls the language customers use to describe the problem, ranks the recurring themes, and refreshes from competitor ads and category content so the system doesn't converge on one angle.

Creative

Generates statics and video, on brand

Turns each theme into ad concepts, then checks every asset against your brand rules and a compliance gate before anything reaches your account.

Publish

Learn

Enforces your kill rule, continuously

Questions

The ones people actually ask.

Will this put my ad account at risk?

The usual cause of trouble is using the Marketing API to bulk-pull data from a live ad account. This system doesn't read performance that way — writes go through the API, reads come from a warehouse fed by a supported data pipeline. That separation is deliberate.

Does AI-generated creative actually work?

Some of it will and most of it won't — that's the point. The value isn't that any single generated ad is brilliant, it's that you can put many angles in front of the market cheaply and let spend data decide, rather than betting a long production cycle on one idea.

What stops it from publishing something embarrassing?

Two gates before anything reaches your account: a brand check on the visuals, and a compliance check on the text that blocks unsubstantiated claims, invented testimonials and anything on your do-not list. Both are configured during Discovery.

What do you need from me?

Access to your ad account and conversion tracking, decisions on your cost thresholds, and about an hour a week reviewing what's being published. Less than managing an agency. More than nothing.
